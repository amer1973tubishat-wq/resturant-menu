Our Story

One grill, one obsession

Ember & Bun started in 2014 as a single charcoal grill behind a hardware store on Kiln Street. We ground our own beef because nobody would sell us the blend we wanted, and we baked our own buns because nothing local held up to the juice.

Eleven years later the recipe hasn't moved. The beef is still ground each morning, the fries are still cut by hand at 6 AM, and the sauce is still made in a stockpot by whoever gets in first.
