Our Story

A GRILL,
A GARAGE,
AND A CITY

Baytna started in 2016 in a converted garage off Rainbow Street, with one flat-top grill and a queue that spilled onto the pavement. Baytna means "our home" — the plan was never a chain, just a place that felt like one.

The beef comes from farms outside Madaba and is ground every morning. The olive oil is pressed in Ajloun. The za'atar is a family blend from a neighbour who has been making it longer than we have been open.

"We buy from people we can drive to."
